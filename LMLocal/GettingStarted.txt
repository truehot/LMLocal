LM Local - Getting Started

1. Open Visual Studio.
2. Open the "LM Local" tool window using one of the following methods:
   - Method A: Open it directly from the top Extensions menu.
   - Method B: In the top menu, go to View ➔ hover over Other Windows ➔ click on LM Local Chat.
3. Make sure your local backend (LM Studio, Ollama, or Jan) is running and a model is loaded.
4. Click the menu icon (…) and open "Settings..." to select your preferred AI Provider, then click the "Test" button to verify the connection.
5. Type your message in the chat box and press Enter or click "Send".
6. (Optional) Click the "+" button to include the entire content of the active document into the conversation.
7. Use the "Stop" button to cancel an active generation if needed.
8. Click the menu icon (…) to wipe the current session history and start fresh.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERFACE TIPS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Use Ctrl+C to copy text and Ctrl+V to paste messages inside the chat window (right-click is disabled).
* Click the "Copy" button in the top-right corner of any AI-generated code block to instantly grab the code.
* Click the collapsible "Thoughts" block at the top of a response to view the AI's internal reasoning process.
* Hover over the top connection/model header to see a tooltip tracking your current token usage and context window limits (supported by providers like LM Studio).
